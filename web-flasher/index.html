CYD PET Radio

Install the web radio with the dancing cat directly from your browser onto your ESP32 CYD.

  1. Use Chrome, Edge, or another desktop browser that supports Web Serial.
  2. Connect the CYD with a USB data cable.
  3. Click "Install", select your board's serial port, and confirm the connection.

On a new install, the flasher may offer to erase the device. Erasing removes the Wi-Fi credentials, station list, volume, and touchscreen calibration. Write down your Wi-Fi credentials before erasing.

After installing, set up Wi-Fi and stations directly on the radio. For CYD hardware notes and setup instructions, see the project repository.
